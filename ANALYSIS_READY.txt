## 🎨 SVG Format

Your `system.svg` must have:

```xml
<!-- Main scattering region (black area) -->
<path d="M ... Z" fill="black" />

<!-- Semi-infinite leads (blue rectangles) -->
<rect ... fill="blue" />
<rect ... fill="blue" />
...
```

**Key Points:**
- **Black** = billiard scattering region (any shape)
- **Blue** = leads (must be rectangles)
- **Pixels** = lattice sites (1:1 mapping)
- **Periodicity** = lead's shorter dimension
